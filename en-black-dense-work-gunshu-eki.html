Crowd (Station)

Year
2024
Medium
Oil on canvas
Dimensions
130.3 × 97.0 cm

A station platform in the morning. Faces are left unpainted; the gathering of people is expressed only through the directions of their bodies and the spaces between them.

Each person is anonymous, yet the rhythm and pressure that arise when they gather may become a portrait of the contemporary city.
