Signal

Year
2025
Medium
Oil on canvas
Dimensions
91.0 × 116.7 cm

A few dozen seconds in the lives of people pausing at a crossing. The colour of the instant the light turns from red to green sits at the centre of the picture.

It depicts the time of waiting, the scattered directions of people's gazes, the feeling that everyone is looking at somewhere slightly different.
