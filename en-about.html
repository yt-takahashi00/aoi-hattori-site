Biography

Aoi Hattori is a painter who takes everyday landscapes and figures as her motifs. Attentive to the relationships of colour, shape and light within familiar scenes, she rebuilds them on the canvas. This site presents her works and exhibition information.

Artist Statement

Even the most unremarkable scene from daily life, when we stop and look again, appears as a rich gathering of colours and shapes. To paint is to hold that momentary impression within the picture, and to confirm it with my own eyes. Through these works, I hope to share new ways of seeing a familiar world.
