Biography

Born in Toyama in 1999. Graduated from the Department of Painting (Oil Painting), Tama Art University, in 2022, and completed the Master's Program in Oil Painting at the same university in 2024. Currently based in Tokyo.

Artist Statement

Hattori creates oil paintings that take as their starting point the history, traditions and social issues that shape Japan. Harmonising elements separated by distance — past and present, Japanese and Western art — she depicts the multilayered nature of 'Japan' with humour and a high level of technical skill.
