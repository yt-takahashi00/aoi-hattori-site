SIMULATION

Year
2026
Medium
Oil on canvas
Dimensions
162.0 × 130.3 cm

Another screen, placed within the screen. The landscape that should be reflected is inverted somewhere along the way, and the boundary between reality and its copy grows ambiguous.

An attempt to re-capture, through painting — the oldest of media — the simulation-like visual experiences we encounter every day: maps, weather forecasts, game screens.
